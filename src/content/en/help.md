## Getting started

### What is Under10?
A map of places where you can eat for under €10 across Europe. Every spot is added and price-checked by people who were there.

### Do I need an account?
No. You can browse the map, spots, menus, photos and the Explore feed as a guest. You need a free account to add spots, like, verify prices, save spots, follow people or post photos, videos and stories.

### Why does the app ask for my location?
To show spots near you and to check you are close to a spot when you verify a price. You can say no and move the map by hand instead.

## Spots and prices

### How do I add a spot?
Tap **+** on the map, place the pin on the restaurant, then enter the name, cuisine, price (under €10), address and city. You can add a short description, photos and a video. You earn 20 points.

### What does "I was there" do?
It confirms that the price is still right. You must be within 1 km of the spot, and you can verify each spot once a day. You earn 2 points. You can't verify a spot you added or a restaurant you own.

### The price is wrong. What do I do?
Open the spot and tap **⋯ → Suggest a new price**. When two different people suggest the same price within 7 days, it is updated automatically, or the restaurant can accept it. A confirmed suggestion earns you 5 points.

### Why did a spot disappear from the map?
Either its price is now €10 or more, or the restaurant marked it as temporarily closed. Closed spots can still be opened from your saved spots.

## Photos, videos and stories

### How long can a video be?
Up to 1 minute and 50 MB. Longer videos are refused before upload.

### How long do stories stay?
24 hours. After that they disappear for everyone and are deleted from our servers.

### How do I delete something I posted?
Open it and tap **⋯ → Delete**. Only you can delete your own photos, videos and stories.

## Points and levels

### How do I earn points?
Add a spot +20, confirmed price suggestion +5, confirmed report +5, "I was there" +2.

### What are the levels?
Newbie (0–49 pts), Explorer (50–199), Local Guide (200–499) and Legend (500+). The leaderboard shows the week, the month and all time, in your city or across Europe.

## For restaurants

### How much does it cost?
7 days free, then €19/month or €190/year. You subscribe inside the Under10 app, and payment goes through the App Store or Google Play. No commission on your sales. Eaters always use Under10 for free.

### How do I get my restaurant on Under10?
Download the app, create a restaurant account, search for your restaurant (or add it if it isn't listed), send a proof that you run it (Kbis, SIRET or invoice) and start your 7-day free trial. We usually review claims within 2 working days.

### How do I cancel?
In your phone's subscription settings. iPhone: **Settings → your name → Subscriptions**. Android: **Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You keep access until the end of the period already paid.

### What happens if I stop paying?
Your page loses the Official badge and the owner tools, but your spot stays on the map as a community spot. Subscribe again in the app at any time to get your page back.

### Can I get a refund?
Payments are handled by Apple or Google, so refunds follow their policies: reportaproblem.apple.com for iPhone, or Google Play for Android.

### What does a boost do?
It shows your spot first in Explore and highlights it on the map for a week. Boosted spots are always labelled Sponsored.

### I'm closed for a while.
Go to **Account → Mark as closed**. Your page shows "Temporarily closed" and leaves the map until you reopen.

## Safety and your account

### How do I report something?
Tap **⋯** on the spot, photo, video, story or profile and choose **Report**. We review every report.

### How do I block someone?
Open their profile and tap **⋯ → Block**. You won't see their content any more.

### How do I delete my account?
**Settings → Delete account**, then type DELETE to confirm. Your profile and everything you posted are deleted permanently. Deleting your account does not cancel a restaurant subscription: cancel it in your phone's settings first. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

### I didn't get my sign-up code.
Check your spam folder and wait 1 minute before asking for a new code. The code has 6 digits.
